I'm going on holiday today, and I've made a plan.

I need a plan because last summer holiday I remember having a mini existential crisis. I was sitting at the table in our cabin and I thought, “What is my purpose? I have no work to do. What is my purpose?!” I was proper freaking out. But then I gathered my thoughts and gave myself a kick up the backside, and realised my purpose for that week was to relax and be present with my family, and that was it.

So before we hop on the plane later today, I've written a plan for myself for this holiday. I have no reason to think about work, because I've spent this week organising things so they'll run without me, and also so that I can hit the ground running on my return. My plan is to relax, have fun, to help my family have fun, to read a book, and to not think about work. 

I'm not even bringing my laptop with me. I feel a bit angsty about that but I know it's the right decision. I will make notes on stuff in a Field Notes notebook, then transfer what I need to my Thymer workspace when I get home. 

I feel prepared. I only hope there is a decent sized coffee mug waiting for me.
